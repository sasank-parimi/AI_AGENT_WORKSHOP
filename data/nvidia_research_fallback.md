## Executive summary

NVIDIA's growth case is tied to continued demand for accelerated computing and AI infrastructure, adoption of its broader hardware-and-software platform, and expansion into additional computing markets. Material risks include customer and supplier concentration, export controls, intense competition, rapid product cycles, and the possibility that infrastructure spending grows more slowly than expected.

## Growth drivers to investigate

1. Demand for accelerated computing in cloud and enterprise data centres.
2. Adoption of NVIDIA's integrated compute, networking and software platform.
3. Expansion into inference, sovereign AI, automotive and other specialised workloads.

## Material risks to investigate

1. Export controls and geopolitical restrictions.
2. Competition from established semiconductor firms and customers' custom silicon.
3. Supply constraints, customer concentration and an AI infrastructure spending slowdown.

## Missing evidence

- Latest quarterly results and guidance.
- Current customer, geographic and supplier concentration figures.
- Independent evidence about demand durability and competing architectures.
- Current regulatory changes and their commercial effect.
