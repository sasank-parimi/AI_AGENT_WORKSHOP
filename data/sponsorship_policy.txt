UWA AI Club Workshop Mock Sponsorship Policy

This document is fictional workshop data, not an official UWA policy.

If a sponsor cannot provide unrestricted cash, the club may request in-kind support that directly covers approved event consumables. A prepaid purchasing method such as a limited-value gift card may be accepted where it is restricted to the agreed event purpose and approved by the event lead.

For any in-kind support, record the sponsor name, date, stated value, intended purpose, who received it, and evidence of how it was used. Keep receipts for purchases made using the support. Do not describe in-kind support as a cash donation.
