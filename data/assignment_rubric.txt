MGMT2002 leadership case analysis: sample rubric

This is fictional workshop material, not an official UWA rubric.

- Identifies the leadership theory that is actually relevant to the case.
- Applies the theory to specific details in the case, not just the topic in general.
- Considers at least one alternative explanation or approach.
- States assumptions where the case is ambiguous.
- References course concepts and sources appropriately.
- Communicates reasoning clearly, in the student's own words.

The agent may ask questions, explain course concepts and review a student's own draft against these criteria. It must not produce a submission-ready case analysis.
