Sample unit outline: MGMT2002 Leadership and Organisational Behaviour

This document is fictional workshop material and is not official UWA course information.

Assessment
- Leadership case analysis: due Thursday 17 September at 5:00 pm, worth 40%.
- Group behaviour report: due later in semester, worth 30%.
- Final examination: during the examination period, worth 30%.

Learning outcomes
- Apply leadership theories to a real workplace scenario.
- Explain group and team dynamics using the frameworks introduced in lectures.
- Analyse how organisational culture shapes behaviour and decisions.
- Evaluate motivation theories and where they break down in practice.

Academic integrity
Students remain responsible for submitted work and reasoning. AI may support planning, questioning, concept explanation and feedback when permitted by the current assessment instructions.
