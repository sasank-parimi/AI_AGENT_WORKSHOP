Sample unit outline — HIST2001

This document is fictional workshop material and is not official UWA course information.

Assessment
- Research essay: 1,500 words, due Wednesday 17 September at 5:00 pm, worth 40%.
- Tutorial contribution: weekly, worth 10%.
- Final examination: during the examination period, worth 50%.

Research essay rubric
- Presents a defensible argument that answers the question.
- Uses relevant evidence from unit readings and lectures.
- Distinguishes evidence from the student's interpretation.
- Cites sources consistently.
- Communicates clearly within the word limit.

Academic integrity
Students remain responsible for the argument, interpretation and submitted prose. AI may support planning, questioning, explanation and feedback when permitted by the current assessment instructions.
