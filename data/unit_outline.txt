Sample unit outline — CITS2200 Data Structures and Algorithms

This document is fictional workshop material and is not official UWA course information.

Assessment
- Algorithms problem set: due Wednesday 16 September at 5:00 pm, worth 20%.
- Programming project: due later in semester, worth 30%.
- Final examination: during the examination period, worth 50%.

Learning outcomes
- Select data structures and algorithms that fit a problem's constraints.
- Explain correctness using the terminology introduced in lectures.
- Analyse time and space complexity.
- Test solutions against representative and edge cases.

Academic integrity
Students remain responsible for submitted solutions and explanations. AI may support planning, questioning, concept explanation and feedback when permitted by the current assessment instructions.
