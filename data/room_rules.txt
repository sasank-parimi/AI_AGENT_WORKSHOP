Sample university study-room rules

These documents are fictional workshop material and are not official UWA policy.

1. A study-room request must include the date, start time, duration, group size and responsible student.
2. Groups must not exceed the room's listed capacity.
3. When accessibility is requested, the selected room must have step-free access and a clear accessible route.
4. Food is not permitted in computer laboratories.
5. A room is not booked until the student confirms the final request.
6. If the availability service fails, the assistant must not claim that a room is available or reserved.
