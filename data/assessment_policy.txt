Sample assessment and extension policy

This document is fictional workshop material and is not official UWA policy.

1. Assessment deadlines are the dates published in the current unit outline.
2. A student should request an extension before the published deadline whenever circumstances allow.
3. The request should identify the affected assessment, requested new date and relevant circumstances.
4. Approval is not automatic. Until a decision is received, the original deadline remains in effect.
5. An AI assistant may help a student organise information or draft a request, but it must not invent circumstances or supporting evidence.
6. If this document does not answer a policy question, the assistant should say so and direct the student to the official unit or university contact.
